# CASE FILE
**Question:** Something odd happened with waste at one café in August. Find the culprit.

## Prime cause
At CAF-CAM-08, the **Ham & Cheese Croissant** experienced a major waste spike during the week of **2026-08-10 to 2026-08-16** [E2]. The weekly waste-to-median-week ratio was **3.29** (wasted 69 units vs. median 21), the highest of any store+product in the August period. Daily detail [E7] shows waste percentages for this product ranged from 20.0% to 37.5% that week, far above the store's normal ~8.7% waste rate. Crucially, **sales stayed normal**—Ham & Cheese Croissant units sold each day were 27, 34, 32, 28, 36, 46, 45 [E7], averaging well above typical levels. This combination—a ratio ≥3 with stable sales—per the rules points to a preparation or handling problem, not low demand.

## Suspects
- **CAF-CAM-08 | Ham & Cheese Croissant, 2026-08-10 to 2026-08-16** — waste 3.29× median with normal sales [E2][E7].

## Ruled out
- **Rain effect**: CAF-CAM-08 has sales_rain_to_dry = 1.01 and waste_pct_rain only slightly above dry (8.84% vs 8.7%) [E3]. Rain did not drive the spike.
- **Public holiday effect**: CAF-CAM-08's sales_holiday_to_normal = 1.0 [E4]. Holidays did not reduce sales.
- **Promotion effect**: No promotion data for CAF-CAM-08 in E5; even where promotions existed (e.g., CAF-HAW-07), sales and waste were nearly identical, ruling promotions out generally.
- **Other store+product combos**: All other August entries in E2 are absent; the only August spike is CAF-CAM-08 | Ham & Cheese Croissant.

## Verdict and recommendation
The **Ham & Cheese Croissant at CAF-CAM-08** had a preparation/handling problem during **2026-08-10 to 2026-08-16**, wasting 3.29× its normal weekly amount while selling normally. Recommend inspecting kitchen prep, storage, or portioning procedures for that product at that café during that week.
