- No other store-specific promotion data are provided for comparison.

## Ruled out
- **Promotion had a significant effect on sales** - ruled out by [E5] showing only a 0.67-unit daily drop (~1 % change).  
- **Promotion had a significant effect on waste** - ruled out by [E5] showing a 0.34 %-point reduction in waste rate, well within normal variance.

## Verdict and recommendation
The mid-May promotion at CAF-HAW-07 did not meaningfully change sales or waste. The evidence indicates the promo days were statistically indistinguishable from normal operation. Managers should treat this promotion as ineffective for driving sales reduction or waste mitigation and consider alternative levers (e.g., adjusting staffing on rain days, revising holiday-related ordering) or longer-run promotional designs with clearer objectives.
