# CASE FILE
**Question:** Something odd happened with waste at one café in August. Find the culprit.

## Prime cause
At **CAF-CAM-08**, the *Ham & Cheese Croissant* experienced a waste spike during **2026-08-10 to 2026-08-16** (weekly waste 69 vs. median 21, ratio **3.29** [E2]). Sales remained normal over that period [E6], but daily waste jumped from an average of **~3 units** outside the period to **9-12 units** during the spike [E7]. The waste percentage climbed from ~9% to **20-37%** [E6][E7]. No rain fell on any of those days [E7], and no promotion was running [E7]. The pattern—normal sales, abrupt waste increase, no weather or promo driver—matches the "waste spike" rule: a ratio ≥3 with stable sales points to a preparation or handling problem, not low demand [Rule 2].

## Suspects
- **CAF-CAM-08 - Ham & Cheese Croissant, 2026-08-10 to 2026-08-16** - the only store/product/week in August with a ≥3 waste ratio [E2]; confirmed by daily detail showing consistent 9-12 units wasted daily despite steady sales [E7].

## Ruled out
- **Rain** - CAF-CAM-08 had 0 mm rain on all 7 spike days [E7]; its sales_rain_to_dry is 1.01 and waste_pct_rain vs. dry are nearly identical (8.84 vs. 8.7) [E3].
- **Public holiday** - CAF-CAM-08’s sales_holiday_to_normal is 1.00 [E4]; the spike falls outside the holiday rows in the data.
- **Promotion** - All 7 spike days had promo=0 [E7]; the store’s promo days (when present) show sales and waste nearly identical to non-promo days, so promotions have no effect [Rule 2].
- **Low demand** - Average daily sales during the spike (35.43) were essentially the same as outside the period (33.98) [E6]; units sold each day ranged 27-46, showing no sales drop.

## Verdict and recommendation
The **Ham & Cheese Croissant** at **CAF-CAM-08** wasted abnormally August 10-16 despite normal sales and no rain or promotion. The evidence points to a **preparation or handling problem** (e.g., over-production, improper storage, or slicing/portioning error). 

**Recommendation:** Investigate kitchen prep logs for that week at CAF-CAM-08; check whether croissant batch sizes were increased, if storage conditions changed, or if a new staff member was handling production. Monitor daily waste % for this product going forward; any recurrence above ~10% should trigger a process review.
