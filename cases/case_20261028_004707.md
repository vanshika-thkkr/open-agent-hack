# CASE FILE
**Question:** Our CBD office cafés had some unusually quiet days this year. What explains them?

## Prime cause
Public holidays cause a substantial drop in sales at CBD-office cafés.  
- [E4] shows that for three CBD-office stores (CAF-BRK-05, CAF-DOC-06, CAF-COL-04) the sales ratio on holidays versus normal days is 0.57, 0.57 and 0.60 respectively - i.e. sales fall to roughly 60 % of their usual level on holidays.  
- The same stores have area_type = cbd_office, so the effect is specific to the café segment in question.

## Suspects
- **Rain** - [E3] indicates that for CBD-office stores (those with outdoor_seating = 0) the sales_rain_to_dry ratio is 0.96, meaning rain has almost no impact on sales.  
- **Promotions** - [E5] compares promo vs. non-promo days for CAF-HAW-07; sales and waste are virtually identical, so promotions are ruled out as a cause of quiet days.

## Ruled out
- Rain effect on CBD-office sales ([E3] sales_rain_to_dry ≈ 1).  
- Promotion effect ([E5] no meaningful sales difference on promo days).  
- General waste spikes (E2) - these relate to waste, not sales volume, and are not specific to CBD-office cafés.

## Verdict and recommendation
The unusually quiet days at the CBD-office cafés are best explained by the occurrence of public holidays, which cut sales to about 60 % of normal levels at those locations.  
**Recommendation:** When planning staffing, inventory, and marketing for CBD-office cafés, account for the predictable sales dip around public holidays. Consider targeted promotions or special offers on holiday periods to mitigate the downturn, or adjust stock levels to reduce waste during those low-sales days.

),
rb AS (
  SELECT b.*, CAST(w.precipitation_mm AS DOUBLE) AS rain_mm
  FROM base b
  JOIN cases.weather_table w ON b.date = w.date
)
SELECT store_id,
       MAX(outdoor) AS outdoor_seating,
       MAX(area_type) AS area_type,
       SUM(CASE WHEN rain_mm > 5 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS rain_rows,
       ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN rain_mm > 5 THEN units_sold END), 2) AS avg_sales_rain,
       ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN rain_mm <= 5 THEN units_sold END), 2) AS avg_sales_dry,
       ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN rain_mm > 5 THEN units_sold END)
             / AVG(CASE WHEN rain_mm <= 5 THEN units_sold END), 2) AS sales_rain_to_dry,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN rain_mm > 5 THEN units_wasted END)
             / SUM(CASE WHEN rain_mm > 5 THEN units_sold END), 2) AS waste_pct_rain,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN rain_mm <= 5 THEN units_wasted END)
             / SUM(CASE WHEN rain_mm <= 5 THEN units_sold END), 2) AS waste_pct_dry

GROUP BY store_id

  JOIN cases.waste_table x
    ON s.date = x.date AND s.store_id = x.store_id AND s.product = x.product
),
hb AS (
  SELECT b.*, CASE WHEN h.hdate IS NULL THEN 0 ELSE 1 END AS is_holiday
  FROM base b
  LEFT JOIN (SELECT DISTINCT date AS hdate FROM cases.holidays_table) h
    ON b.date = h.hdate
)
SELECT store_id,
       MAX(area_type) AS area_type,
       SUM(is_holiday) AS holiday_rows,
       ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN is_holiday = 1 THEN units_sold END), 2) AS avg_sales_holiday,
       ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN is_holiday = 0 THEN units_sold END), 2) AS avg_sales_normal,
       ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN is_holiday = 1 THEN units_sold END)
             / AVG(CASE WHEN is_holiday = 0 THEN units_sold END), 2) AS sales_holiday_to_normal
FROM hb
GROUP BY store_id
ORDER BY sales_holiday_to_normal ASC
